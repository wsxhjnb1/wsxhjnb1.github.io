About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at the University of Central Florida. My research focuses on efficient large language model inference, especially KV-cache quantization and GPU kernel optimization.

I work across methods and systems: learning compact KV representations, developing fused attention kernels, and integrating scoring optimizations into vLLM. Previously, I worked on LLM inference at Hithink RoyalFlush.

Research interests

  • KV-cache quantization
  • CUDA & fused attention
  • LLM inference systems

Projects

OrbitCall

CUDA / vLLM

An opt-in vLLM 0.29 plugin for scoring finite JSON key-order tool-call families. It preserves native LM-head projection shapes and complete JSON/EOS likelihoods while reducing normalization, ranking and CPU transfer.

1.08× complete two-round Agent speedup. Qwen2.5-3B · DGX Spark (GB10) · six paired blocks · eight fixed workflows · versus native full scoring.

CodeResults

Attention decoding with quantized KV caches

GPU KERNELS

Fused on-the-fly KV dequantization, attention scoring, online softmax and value accumulation. Packed K/V are reconstructed in registers, avoiding full-precision KV materialization in GPU memory.

40% faster inference in the measured setup. Llama 2 7B · one NVIDIA H100 · versus a Hugging Face implementation.
